Before updating, please **back-up** your world. This is a beta version and 
may contain some instability. Greatly appreciate any bug reports on the 
[Github](https://github.com/ChefMooon/frights-delight/issues), I'll be 
actively watching the issues page to resolve any bugs as soon as possible.
This mod is compatible with Minecraft 1.21.5 and Farmer's Delight Refabricated
([details](https://github.com/ChefMooon/frights-delight/wiki#compatible-versions)).

Minecraft 1.21.5 Fabric Release!

### Added

- New Food
    - Rotten Flesh Pie (+Slice)
    - Slime Apple Pie (+Slice)
    - Spider Eye Pie (+Slice)
    - Ghast Tear Pie (+Slice)
    - Soul Berry Cheesecake (+Slice)
    - Wither Berry Cheesecake (+Slice)
    - Cobweb Pie (+Slice)
- Origins Tags
    - Origins Meat: Slice of Rotten Flesh Pie, Slice of Slime-Apple Pie, Slice of Spider Eye Pie
    - Ignore Diet: Ghast Tear Pie Slice, Cobweb Pie Slice
- Create Tag
    - Upright on Belt: Rotten Flesh Pie, Slime-Apple Pie, Spider Eye Pie, Ghast Tear Pie, Soul Berry Cheesecake, Wither Berry Cheesecake, Cobweb Pie

### Fixed

- Added missing Slime Apple Soup to Master of Fright advancement
- Adding missing translations for mod tags

### Changed

- [1.20.1+] Bone Shards now require a short charge up before they can be thrown.
